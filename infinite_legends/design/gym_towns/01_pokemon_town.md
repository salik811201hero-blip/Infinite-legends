# Aetheria Gym Town 01 — Pokémon-themed town

**Status:** Initial design entry — not yet a playable map  
**Place in progression:** First themed Gym Town, after the Lumenport opening  
**Theme:** The original Pokémon adventure experience

## Core identity

This town celebrates the classic Pokémon world: becoming a Trainer, meeting Pokémon, exploring routes, visiting shops, and challenging a Gym. It should feel like a welcoming, lively Pokémon town rather than a parody or a copy of one existing official town.

## Visual direction

- A bright, welcoming town with a central Pokémon-style plaza.
- A Pokémon Center and Poké Mart as recognizable town services.
- Trainer School or practice field where NPCs teach battle basics.
- A notice board showing local events, trainer tips, and Gym information.
- A route exit leading toward the next part of Aetheria.

## NPC ideas

- A veteran Trainer who shares stories about travelling across regions.
- A beginner Trainer who asks for help understanding type matchups.
- A Pokémon Center worker who explains healing and party management.
- A local researcher who mentions that Pokémon from many regions are appearing in Aetheria.
- A Gym guide who explains the Gym challenge without revealing its puzzle.

All NPCs and dialogue should be original to Infinite Legends.

## Side-quest hooks

1. **A Trainer's First Partner:** Help a new Trainer find a lost item before their first journey. Reward: a useful early-game item.
2. **A Visitor from Far Away:** Find a visiting Trainer who has travelled from another region and complete a small battle or fetch quest. Reward: an item or regional lore.
3. **Type Matchup Practice:** Complete a short optional tutorial battle. Reward: a small supply of healing items.

## Gym

- This is intended to be the first Gym Town.
- Gym Leader, type, team, badge name, puzzle, and exact difficulty are **not decided yet**.
- The Gym should feel like a classic Pokémon Gym challenge, while still having an original Aetheria identity.

## Gameplay requirements

- Explore the town and enter its key buildings.
- Talk to NPCs and trigger at least one optional side quest.
- Visit the Pokémon Center and Poké Mart.
- Reach the Gym and receive the challenge introduction.
- Connect the town to its incoming and outgoing routes.
- Save and reload while in town without losing progress.

## Open decisions

- Final town name.
- Gym type and Gym Leader.
- Whether the town's main visual reference is a classic starter town, a large Pokémon League hub, or a blend.
- Which optional NPC quests should be included in the first playable version.

## Implementation note

This document records the first themed Gym Town concept. It does not mean the town map, graphics, events, scripts, or a playable ROM have been implemented. Those must be created and tested in the game engine separately.
